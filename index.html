Useful signals, with their limits in view.

Srcly combines broad source metrics with language-aware analysis where available. The supported signals vary by language and tool setup; a metric that does not apply is N/A, never zero.

Data-flow analysis covers TypeScript, JavaScript, and Python. It runs with Node.js 18 or newer. Without Node, the rest of Srcly still works and data flow shows the reason it was not analyzed.
